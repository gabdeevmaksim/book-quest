QUEST BOOK

Your choices. Your dice. Your story.

A free, browser-based choose-your-own-adventure RPG. Every tale branches on what you decide and the roll of the dice — with stats, monsters, and loot along the way. And you can conjure a brand-new adventure on any theme, in your language, at your level.

▶ Play now — it's free ☕ Support on Ko-fi

What awaits you

📖

Branching adventures

Hand-crafted and AI-made stories where every choice forks the path to a different ending.

🎲

Real RPG mechanics

Roll your hero's stats, beat dice checks, fight monsters, find items, and heal your wounds.

✨

Make your own world

Type a theme — pirate ghost ship, Norse myth, haunted Mars — and the engine writes a full quest.

🗣️

Any language, any level

Play in your language at a CEFR level from A1 to C2 — a fun way to practise while you adventure.
